About the lab

Mission

The Spinal Surgery Outcomes Laboratory aims to improve patient outcomes through surgical insights and technological innovation.

Our research spans a broad range of spine pathologies and treatments, including lateral lumbar interbody fusion (LLIF), spine oncology, cervical spine fractures, and minimizing wound-healing complications after surgery. Current projects include automated classification of C1 fracture imaging and a comparison of IRRISEPT with standard of care for preventing wound complications.

The laboratory is led by Rahul A. Sastry, MD, MPH, and collaborates with Jared Fridley, MD, of the University of Texas at Austin. Members include neurosurgery residents, medical and graduate students, research assistants, and undergraduate interns who work on retrospective cohort studies, database analyses, and case reports.
