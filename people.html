Rahul A. Sastry, MD, MPH

Principal Investigator · Neurosurgeon

Dr. Sastry is a neurosurgeon at Brown University Health specializing in complex spine surgery. He completed his neurosurgery residency at Brown University and Rhode Island Hospital, followed by a fellowship in complex spine surgery at the University of California, San Francisco. His research focuses on clinical outcomes, health systems research, and international neurotrauma and neurosurgery.
